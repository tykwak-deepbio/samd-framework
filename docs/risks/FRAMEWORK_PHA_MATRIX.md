## 1. Scope & Methodology
Under **IEC 62304 Clause 7.1.1** and **ISO 14971 Clause 5.4**, every software requirement item (`FR-*`) defined in the Software Requirements Specification (`docs/srs/`) must be systematically evaluated to determine whether its software failure mode or behavior could contribute to a hazardous situation.

- **Safety-Related (SR)**: A defect, deviation, or failure in this function can directly or indirectly lead to an unvetted regulatory release, clinical diagnostic error, cybersecurity breach, or patient safety compromise.
- **Non-Safety-Related (NSR)**: Pure development workflow efficiency or convenience feature where a failure is fail-safe and cannot compromise clinical safety, DHF integrity, or regulatory compliance (requires formal justification).
